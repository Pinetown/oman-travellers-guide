Our Time
in Oman

A week of white walls, camels in the shade, hot sand and long orange evenings — from a small camera and no particular plan.

17 frames from the twenty-six we brought home.

Oman in March 2008: a coast road with the sea along one side, thorn trees with camels parked underneath them, a village that looked painted onto the mountainside, and evenings that turned the whole sky orange. The dates stamped in the corner are the camera's. Everything else in them is ours.

The coast

Sea along one side, headlands, and a town seen from up on the road.

Camels

Parked in the shade, then out on the open sand.

White walls

A town that seemed to have been painted onto the hill.

Evening

Three evenings, all of them orange.

17 frames chosen from the twenty-six we brought home — the leftovers are mostly more camels. Several of these also appear in the guide; the rest live here.
